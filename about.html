Our Story

Founded in 2015, GTEXTLAND celebrated its 10th anniversary on October 10, 2025 — a decade of making luxury land ownership accessible through fully serviced estates, verified titles, and flexible payment structures across Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Ogun State, and Port Harcourt.

Every estate features gated communities, paved roads, drainage, perimeter fencing, and recreational areas — giving you a ready-to-build plot from day one with Certificate of Occupancy or equivalent title.
