A Diaspora Investor's Guide to Buying Land in Nigeria

Secure your plot from anywhere in the world — with confidence and verified documentation.

Millions of Nigerians living abroad want to invest back home — but stories of land scams and unclear titles make many hesitant. The good news: with the right developer and a clear process, remote land purchase can be safe, transparent, and straightforward.

Step 1: Choose a Verified Developer

Work with established companies that provide clear documentation — Certificate of Occupancy (C of O), survey plans, and deed of assignment. GTEXTLAND estates across Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, and beyond come with fully verified titles.

Step 2: Virtual Consultation & Estate Selection

Book a free video consultation with our advisors. We'll walk you through available estates, pricing, payment plans, and projected appreciation — all from your phone or laptop.

Step 3: Secure Payment & Documentation

Once you select a plot, we guide you through payment (including international transfers) and begin the documentation process. You'll receive copies of all legal documents for your records.

Step 4: Optional Site Visit

When you visit Nigeria, we arrange a guided tour of your estate. Many diaspora clients combine a family trip with a site visit — it's the best way to see your investment firsthand.
