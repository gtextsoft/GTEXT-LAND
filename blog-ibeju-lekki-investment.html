Ibeju Lekki: Why Investors Are Rushing to This Lagos Corridor

Infrastructure megaprojects are reshaping one of Africa's most valuable real estate zones.

Ibeju Lekki has transformed from a quiet coastal stretch into one of the most talked-about investment corridors in West Africa. Anchored by the Dangote Refinery, Lekki Deep Sea Port, and the Lekki Free Trade Zone, the area is attracting businesses, workers, and residents at an unprecedented pace.

The Infrastructure Effect

Major infrastructure doesn't just create jobs — it creates demand for housing, retail, and services. Every new factory, port facility, and logistics hub needs people nearby, and those people need places to live. Land values in corridors connected to these projects have historically seen sharp appreciation within 5–10 years of completion.
