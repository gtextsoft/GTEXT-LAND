Why Land Banking Is Nigeria's Smartest Long-Term Investment

How strategic plot acquisition builds wealth that compounds over decades.

Land banking — the practice of acquiring undeveloped land in high-growth areas and holding it for appreciation — has created more millionaires in Nigeria than almost any other asset class. Unlike buildings that depreciate and require maintenance, land in the right location tends to appreciate as infrastructure, population, and commerce expand around it.

What Makes Land Different?

Land is finite. As cities grow outward, well-positioned plots become increasingly scarce. In corridors like Ibeju Lekki, Epe, and Kurudu, government-backed infrastructure projects are transforming formerly rural areas into prime residential and commercial zones — and early investors are seeing multiples on their original purchase price.
